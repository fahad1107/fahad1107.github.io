How eBay Dropshipping Works in Germany

The eBay dropshipping model is straightforward: you list products on eBay.de that you do not physically hold in stock. When a buyer purchases, you order that item from a wholesale supplier who ships directly to the buyer. Your profit is the difference between the eBay sale price and what you paid the supplier.

No warehouse. No upfront bulk purchase. No packing products yourself. The inventory risk sits with the supplier, not with you. Your job is to find the right products, price them correctly, create listings that convert, and manage the customer experience.

💡
Why Germany specifically? eBay.de is one of the most active eCommerce marketplaces in Europe. German buyers are reliable payers, have high average order values, and consistent purchasing behaviour across the year. For a dropshipping business that relies on volume, that is a powerful market to operate in.

The legal framework for this in Germany is clear: register a Gewerbe, handle VAT correctly, issue compliant invoices, and follow eBay’s seller policies. Do these correctly and you have a fully legitimate business. Miss them and you create serious tax and account risk. We cover the legal setup in detail in Is eBay Dropshipping Legal in Germany?

7-Step Setup Roadmap — From Zero to First Sale

Follow this sequence. Many beginners skip steps 1 and 2 and go straight to listing — which creates legal and account problems immediately.

1
Week 1

Register Your Gewerbe

Visit your local Gewerbeamt (or do it online in some cities). Select “Einzelunternehmen” (sole trader) as your business form. The activity code covers online retail (Einzelhandel via Internet, Code 47.91). Cost: €20–60. You receive a Gewerbeanmeldung confirmation within days. Keep this — you need it for the next step.

2
Week 1

Apply for Your USt-IdNr (VAT ID)

Apply via the Bundeszentralamt fuer Steuern online portal using your Gewerbeanmeldung. Free. Takes 2–4 weeks. In the meantime, your Finanzamt will send you a Steuernummer (tax number) which you can use as an interim identifier in invoices. The USt-IdNr appears on all customer invoices and your eBay business account.

3
Week 1-2

Create or Convert Your eBay Business Account

Create a new eBay.de account or convert your existing personal account to a Business account. Add your business name, address, USt-IdNr (once received), and bank account details. Complete the eBay seller identity verification. A properly configured business account is the legal foundation for all your selling activity.

4
Week 1-2

Set Up Accounting Software

Lexware Office with SKR04 is the recommended setup for German eBay sellers. Configure it to automatically generate compliant invoices for every transaction — including your USt-IdNr, correct VAT amounts, and sequential invoice numbers. Set a reminder for your first Umsatzsteuervoranmeldung deadline.

5
Week 2-3

Source Your Suppliers

Find reliable wholesale or dropshipping suppliers who can ship in accurate, reasonable timeframes and provide real tracking numbers. Not Amazon, Zalando, or other retail platforms — using retail arbitrage violates eBay’s dropshipping policy and gets accounts banned. Good supplier relationships are the most valuable operational asset in this business.

6
Week 2-3

Create Optimised eBay Listings

German-language titles with relevant search keywords. Accurate category selection. Realistic, conservative shipping timeframes — if your supplier takes 7 days, list 10–12 days delivery. High-quality product images. Clear, specific product descriptions with specifications. Getting this right from the start builds your seller metrics and organic search visibility.

7
Week 3+

Set Up VPS and Automation

Once you have your first 20–50 listings live and initial orders coming in, move from manual to automated. Get a Windows VPS in Frankfurt for a German IP, install repricing and order management tools, and configure monitoring. This is the transition from a side project to a scalable operation. See our VPS setup guide for the complete walkthrough.

How to Find Profitable Products for eBay Germany

Product selection is the single biggest lever in eBay dropshipping. The right products sell consistently. The wrong products sit unnoticed, accumulate defect returns, and drain working capital.

Categories That Perform Well on eBay.de

  • Household and garden products — consistent year-round demand, moderate competition, good margins
  • Tools and hardware — German buyers invest in quality tools, higher average order values
  • Electronics accessories — phone cases, cables, chargers — high volume, fast turnover
  • Pet supplies — Germany has one of the highest pet ownership rates in Europe, very consistent demand
  • Automotive accessories — car-owning Germany is a strong market for auto-related accessories
  • Sports and outdoor — seasonal peaks but strong year-round baseline

Categories to Approach Carefully

  • Electronics (phones, laptops) — high return rates, complex warranty obligations under German consumer law
  • Fashion and clothing — sizing issues create high return rates under eBay’s buyer protection
  • Food and perishables — complex German food safety regulations
  • Brand-name items without authorisation — listing branded products without reseller authorisation risks VERO (Verified Rights Owner) removal

Product Research Tools

Use data to validate demand before listing. Tools like eBay Terapeak (available in your eBay seller hub) show what has actually sold, at what price, and with what velocity over the past 90 days. Third-party tools like Zik Analytics provide deeper product research specifically for eBay dropshipping. Never list based on gut feel — validate with data first.

Creating eBay Listings That Actually Sell

A listing is your primary sales tool on eBay. Most beginners underinvest in this and wonder why their products do not sell. Here is what separates listings that convert from ones that do not:

The eBay.de Title Formula

eBay’s search algorithm is keyword-driven. Your title is the most important ranking factor. A strong German eBay title follows this structure:

✍️
[Brand/Type] [Product Name] [Key Spec] [Material/Feature] [Secondary Spec] [Use Case] — max 80 characters

Example: Edelstahl Gartenschlauch Schlauchbox 20m ausziehbar Wasserschlauch selbstaufrollend
  • Include all searchable specifications — size, material, colour, capacity, compatibility
  • German language required — German buyers search in German, not English
  • No promotional language — "TOP ANGEBOT!!" style titles are penalised by eBay’s algorithm
  • No special characters — asterisks, exclamation marks in titles reduce visibility
  • Use all 80 characters — every unused character is a lost keyword opportunity

Delivery Time Setting

This is the single most impactful decision on your seller metrics. If your supplier takes 7–10 days, list 10–14 day delivery. Never list a shorter window than you can reliably meet. A late shipment defect has a much larger negative impact on your account health than a slightly longer listed delivery time has on conversion rate.

The 8 Mistakes That Kill New eBay Businesses in Germany

🚫 1. No Gewerbe Before Selling

Selling commercially without registration is illegal in Germany. The Finanzamt monitors this. Back-tax demands and fines can follow.

🚫 2. Ignoring VAT

Collecting eBay sale revenue without reporting VAT creates tax debt that compounds with penalties. The Finanzamt pursues this retroactively.

🚫 3. Amazon to eBay Arbitrage

Buying from Amazon and dropshipping to eBay buyers explicitly violates eBay’s policy. This leads directly to account suspension.

🚫 4. Fake Tracking Numbers

Uploading placeholder tracking to close orders is fraud under eBay’s policy. Permanent suspension with no appeal path.

🚫 5. Unrealistic Delivery Promises

Listing 2–3 day shipping when your supplier takes 10+ days creates defect rates that push you below eBay seller standards instantly.

🚫 6. No VAT-Compliant Invoices

German law (§14 UStG) requires specific invoice elements. Missing your USt-IdNr or incorrect VAT amounts creates audit risk.

🚫 7. Scaling Without a VPS

Managing 100+ orders manually or via home laptop is impossible without errors. Missing orders, late processing, and customer complaints compound fast.

🚫 8. Choosing Cheap Over Compliant Services

Unregulated cheap eBay management services use policy-violating methods. Short-term sales, permanent account suspension long-term.
