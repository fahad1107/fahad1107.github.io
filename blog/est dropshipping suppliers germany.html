Why Your Supplier Determines Your Success — or Your Suspension

Let's start with the most important truth about eBay dropshipping in Germany that most guides skip: your supplier is your account's biggest risk factor. In 2026, eBay Germany uses sophisticated detection to identify packages shipped from non-business sources — specifically from Amazon, Zalando, and other retail marketplaces.

Every single eBay account suspension I have analysed in the German market came back to one of three supplier problems:

  • Retail arbitrage — buying from Amazon or Zalando and sending directly to buyers
  • No supplier agreement — no signed contract to prove wholesale sourcing when eBay demands it
  • Unreliable shipping — late deliveries from slow Asian suppliers destroying account health metrics

A proper wholesale supplier gives you three things retail can never provide: a signed dropshipping agreement (your legal protection), invoices showing your business name (not a consumer receipt), and reliable tracked shipping with realistic delivery windows that keep your defect rate clean.

⚠️
The 2026 Reality: eBay Germany's policy explicitly states that sourcing from retail or marketplace platforms is prohibited. Accounts caught doing this receive suspensions without warning. The suppliers in this guide are wholesale-only sources that comply with eBay's Dropshipping Policy.

What eBay Germany Requires from Suppliers

Before we discuss specific suppliers, you need to understand exactly what eBay Germany expects. The 2026 eBay Dropshipping Policy requires that sellers who use the dropshipping model must:

  1. Source only from wholesale suppliers or manufacturers — not from retail platforms
  2. Be the seller of record — you are responsible for the item even though you do not physically ship it
  3. Ensure accurate delivery times — your listed delivery window must be achievable
  4. Handle all returns — even if this means coordinating a return to the supplier
  5. Maintain account health metrics — defect rate under 2%, late shipment under 10%

When eBay investigates an account, they can request supplier documentation. This means you need, at minimum: a signed dropshipping agreement naming your business, and historical invoices showing your business as the purchaser. Consumer receipts from Amazon do not qualify.

✅
What eBay wants to see: A B2B invoice from a registered wholesale business to your registered business (Primashop, your GmbH, or your Einzelunternehmen). The invoice should include your business address, both VAT numbers, and clearly show a wholesale price — not a consumer retail price.

The 4 Types of Suppliers for eBay Germany

Not all wholesale suppliers are structured the same way. Understanding the four types helps you choose the right approach for your target product category and business stage.

Type 1: European Wholesale Distributors

These are established B2B companies, typically based in Germany, Netherlands, UK, or Poland, who distribute products from manufacturers to retailers. They sell in bulk but many have dropshipping programmes. This is the gold standard for eBay Germany — fast EU shipping, proper invoices, professional terms.

Type 2: UK-Based Wholesale Suppliers

After Brexit, UK suppliers operate outside the EU, but many have established EU distribution hubs. UK suppliers often have exceptional product ranges in home goods, tools, pet supplies, and automotive accessories — all strong categories for eBay Germany. Shipping is 2–5 days to Germany and they typically offer formal dropshipping agreements.

Type 3: Manufacturer Direct (MOQ-Flexible)

Some manufacturers will work with dropshippers directly if you establish a business relationship and agree to a minimum monthly volume. The margins here are significantly better — 35–55% vs 20–30% from distributors — but it requires more relationship-building upfront.

Type 4: Specialised Dropshipping Platforms (EU-Compliant)

Platforms like Ankorstore, Orderchamp, or Faire connect retailers with European brands offering dropshipping terms. These are quicker to set up than direct supplier relationships, but margins tend to be thinner and you share the supplier with many other dropshippers — increasing competition on eBay.

How to Find and Vet Suppliers Yourself — The Professional Method

The suppliers that work best are never on public dropshipping lists. They are found through direct research. Here is the exact method used by professional eBay Germany operations:

1

Start with Wer Liefert Was (WLW)

WLW is Germany's B2B supplier directory — the equivalent of ThomasNet for the German market. Search your target product category in German. Filter for German, Austrian, and Swiss suppliers first. Visit their websites directly to assess professionalism.

2

Check Europages for EU-Wide Coverage

Europages covers 26 European countries. Filter by product category and country. Look for suppliers with a proper B2B website, minimum order information, and a contact form — not just a phone number. Professional suppliers have professional digital presences.

3

Call or Email with a Professional Introduction

Do not use the enquiry form. Email directly: introduce your business (use your registered business name), explain you are a commercial eBay seller looking for a dropshipping arrangement, state your approximate monthly volume, and ask about their dropshipping terms. You will hear back from 20–40% of suppliers contacted.

4

Request a Sample Order Before Committing

Before signing anything, order a sample of the product you plan to sell. Check packaging quality, shipping time, tracking reliability, and whether the delivery matches your eBay listing's delivery promise. One test order saves you from a bad supplier relationship.

5

Review Their Invoices and VAT Setup

The invoice you receive from your supplier must show: your business name, your business address, their VAT number, your VAT number (if registered), a proper item description, and a net price. Consumer-looking invoices (no VAT number, no B2B pricing structure) will not pass eBay's documentation check.

6

Sign a Formal Dropshipping Agreement

Before placing your first eBay order using their products, get a signed agreement. This protects you legally and is your proof of a compliant supplier relationship if eBay ever audits your account. See the next section for exactly what this agreement needs to contain.

The Dropshipping Agreement — What It Must Include

This is where most dropshippers cut corners and pay for it later. Your supplier agreement is not just a formality — it is your legal protection with eBay, the Finanzamt, and in any consumer dispute. A proper agreement must include:

  • Both business names and addresses — your registered business and the supplier's registered business
  • Both VAT numbers — essential for B2B invoicing under German VAT law
  • Clear statement that you are the retailer / Händler — you are buying wholesale and reselling
  • Pricing structure — wholesale price list or confirmation that you receive trade prices
  • Shipping terms — who ships, from where, typical delivery time, carrier used
  • Return and warranty handling — clear process for defective items
  • Neutral packaging clause — confirmation that shipments will arrive in plain or your-branded packaging, NOT in the supplier's retail packaging with retail price labels
  • Confidentiality — supplier's identity remains confidential if you resell as your own brand
