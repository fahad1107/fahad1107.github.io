What Is Amazon-to-eBay Dropshipping?

Amazon-to-eBay dropshipping is the practice of listing products on eBay, then purchasing them from Amazon or Zalando or Otto and having them shipped directly to your eBay buyer. You profit from the price difference. In 2026, it is the fastest route to permanent eBay account suspension.

Alert
The reality in 2026: eBay algorithm actively detects retail arbitrage patterns. Accounts caught doing this receive restrictions within weeks and permanent suspension shortly after. There is no workaround.

Why It Fails in 2026

  • Tracking cross-referencing: Amazon carrier tracking data is cross-referenceable. eBay identifies patterns of tracking numbers originating from Amazon logistics centers
  • Package content detection: Buyers frequently report receiving Amazon-branded packages. Each report triggers an account review
  • Price monitoring: When buyers find the same item cheaper on Amazon, they open Not As Described cases that spike your defect rate
  • Collapsing margins: Amazon dynamic pricing means the retail price can shift at any moment, eliminating your margin on orders already committed

eBay Policy: The Clear Prohibition

eBay official 2026 Dropshipping Policy states: Listing an item on eBay and then purchasing the item from another retailer or marketplace that ships directly to your customer is not permitted. eBay requires sourcing from wholesale suppliers or manufacturers with a direct supplier relationship. You must be able to produce a wholesale supplier agreement if eBay requests it.

Warning
Already doing this? Stop immediately and transition to compliant wholesale sourcing. Continuing after eBay policy is this explicit creates additional liability.

How eBay Detects Retail Arbitrage in 2026

  • Tracking pattern analysis: Tracking numbers from Amazon, Zalando, and Otto logistics networks are flagged automatically
  • Buyer complaint velocity: Clusters of Amazon box complaints from multiple buyers trigger immediate review
  • Return address analysis: Return addresses pointing to Amazon fulfilment centers or retail warehouses
  • Supplier invoice requests: eBay can request supplier documentation at any time. Retail receipts from Amazon do not qualify as wholesale documentation

How to Pivot to Wholesale Sourcing

1

Identify wholesale suppliers in your categories

Trade directories, trade shows like Ambiente and Paperworld, and direct manufacturer outreach. Ask for dropshipping terms and minimum order requirements.

2

Establish a formal supplier agreement

Get a written dropshipping agreement showing your business name, trading terms, and authorisation to sell their products. This is your eBay compliance documentation.

3

Set up neutral fulfilment

Ensure your supplier ships in plain packaging or with your brand labels, never in their own retail branding that confuses buyers.

4

Negotiate payment terms

Work toward net-30 payment terms as your relationship grows. This reduces the working capital float requirement significantly.
