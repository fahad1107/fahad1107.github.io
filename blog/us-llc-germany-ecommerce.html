The US LLC Tax Myth Explained

A US LLC has become a popular concept in the German online business community, promoted heavily on social media with claims like "pay 0% tax on your dropshipping income" or "the Finanzamt cannot touch you." These claims are categorically false for German residents.

Alert
The hard truth: If you live in Germany, you pay German taxes on your worldwide income regardless of where your business entity is incorporated. A US LLC does not change your German tax residency status.

How German Tax Law Treats a US LLC

Germany taxes its residents on their worldwide income (unbeschrankte Steuerpflicht). When you as a German resident form a US LLC:

  • The German Finanzamt treats a US LLC as a transparente Personengesellschaft - a pass-through entity whose income flows directly to you personally
  • All LLC profits are attributed to you as German-taxable personal income
  • You still pay German income tax up to 45% on every euro of LLC profit
  • You still owe German VAT on all taxable transactions
  • You still need a German Gewerbe if the business activities constitute commercial trade in Germany
Warning
Double taxation risk: You may also owe US state taxes depending on where the LLC is registered, creating potential double taxation exposure. Wyoming and Delaware LLCs have state-level nuances but none eliminate German tax liability.

When a US LLC Actually Makes Sense

A US LLC genuinely helps when:

  • You are scaling to the US market - selling on Amazon.com, eBay.com, or Walmart.com and need US banking and payment processing
  • You have significant B2B needs - certain US payment processors and wholesale accounts require a US entity
  • You plan to physically relocate to the US - if you genuinely move to the US, the tax picture changes completely
  • Complex multi-entity holding structures - well beyond early-stage eBay businesses

The Real Risks for German Residents

  • Finanzamt investigation: German tax authorities are aware of the US LLC trend. Unexplained offshore structures trigger audits
  • Back taxes plus interest: The Finanzamt can demand back taxes plus 6% annual interest plus potential criminal charges for tax evasion
  • Bank account problems: German banks are increasingly cautious about accounts connected to offshore entities without clear tax compliance documentation
  • Legal setup costs: Setting up a US LLC correctly costs 1,500-3,000 EUR plus upfront for zero German tax benefit

Better Alternatives for German eBay Sellers

  • Einzelunternehmen (sole trader) with proper accounting: The standard structure for eBay sellers. Simple, legal, and optimised for German tax law
  • UG (haftungsbeschrankt): Germany mini-GmbH. Limited liability, starts from 1 EUR capital, corporate tax rate of 15% plus solidarity surcharge plus trade tax. Worth considering when profit consistently exceeds 60,000 EUR per year
  • GmbH: Full German limited company. Appropriate for larger operations with multiple employees or investors
  • Legitimate tax deductions: Home office, VPS costs, software, professional development. A good Steuerberater can reduce your effective tax rate significantly within the legal framework
